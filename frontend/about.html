About JukeBob

We're on a mission to revolutionize how people experience music at venues. JukeBob bridges the gap between music lovers and artists, creating interactive experiences that put the power of the playlist back where it belongs – with the people.

Our Story

JukeBob was born from a simple frustration: walking into a bar and hearing the same tired playlist while your favorite songs sat unplayed. We thought, "What if everyone could have a say in what plays next?"

Founded in 2025, we started with a simple idea: democratize the DJ booth. Today, we're building India's first crowd-powered music platform, connecting venues, artists, and music lovers in ways never seen before.

What Makes Us Different

We're not just another music app. We're creating a new social experience around music. From interactive jukeboxes to artist tipping, from AI-powered DJing to live event discovery – JukeBob is where technology meets the party.
